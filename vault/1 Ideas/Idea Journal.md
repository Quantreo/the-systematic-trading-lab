# Idea Journal

> **What this is** : one entry per idea, the day you meet it. Not a to-do list: a queue, newest first.
> **Open it** : at the end of every reading slot (add an entry), at the start of every derivation slot (pick an entry that is a week old and still looks good).
> **Rule** : write the mechanism in one sentence, or do not write the idea at all. Here the sentence is a filter, not an analysis: the full answer (when it works, when it breaks, what kills it) belongs to Pass 1 of the strategy sheet.

**Entry format** : date · title / mechanism in one sentence / `family` · source · **status** · revisit date · sheet
**Status** : new → reading → derived → sheet → dropped

The entries marked **new** or **reading** are open: nobody has built them. Pick one, and it is yours.

### 2026-09 · FX carry on the G10 majors
Currencies with high interest rates pay the rate differential to whoever holds them; the holder is paid for the crash risk in risk-off episodes.
`carry` · derived from [[Koijen 2018 Carry]] (universe brick) · **derived** · revisit 2026-10

### 2026-09 · Commodity carry from the futures curve
A commodity in backwardation pays you to hold the front contract: hedgers who need it now accept a higher price. Long backwardation, short contango.
`carry` · [[Koijen 2018 Carry]] · **new** · revisit 2026-10

### 2026-08 · Month-end cash needs in equity and bond markets
Institutions that must pay out cash at month-end (pensions, fund redemptions) sell just before: prices are temporarily pushed down around those dates, then recover.
`seasonality / flows` · Etula, Rinne, Suominen, Vaittinen (2020), *Dash for Cash* · **reading** · revisit 2026-10

### 2026-08 · Month-end USD flows at the London 4 pm fix
Foreign investors who hedge their US equity holdings rebalance the hedge at month-end, at the fix: a flow with a known date, a known time, and a direction you can estimate from the month's equity move.
`seasonality / flows` · Melvin and Prins (2015), *Equity hedging and exchange rates at the London 4 p.m. fix* · **new** · revisit 2026-10

### 2026-08 · Token unlocks in crypto
Teams and early investors receive tokens on a published schedule and sell part of them: a supply shock with a known date.
`event-driven` · unlock calendars (Token Unlocks, CryptoRank) · **new** : check first that the short side is tradable on the names concerned · revisit 2026-10

### 2026-08 · Weekend effect in crypto (Friday close → Monday open)
If it existed, the market makers who trade the weekend would have taken it; short sample, no reason behind it.
`seasonality` · several blogs · **dropped** : already arbitraged, no mechanism

### 2026-08 · Cross-sectional momentum, crypto top 20
Relative strength persists inside a highly correlated asset class; a long/short book captures it and neutralises the market bet.
`trend (XS)` · derived from [[Moskowitz Ooi Pedersen 2012 Time Series Momentum]] (universe + signal bricks) · **sheet** · [[Crypto XS Momentum]]

### 2026-07 · Extreme speculator positioning in FX futures (COT)

`reversal / positioning` · CFTC Commitments of Traders · **reading** · revisit 2026-10
