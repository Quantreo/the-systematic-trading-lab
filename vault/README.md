# The Systematic Lab : research vault

Your research desk: everything you need to go from an idea to a strategy in a portfolio, in one place.

**Open it** : in Obsidian, *File → Open folder as vault*, and choose this folder. Come back to this page whenever you are lost.

## The pipeline

```
 1 Ideas  →  2 Strategies  →  4 Portfolio
 read,        Pass 1, build,     the book,
 derive       test, verdict      live monitoring
                   ↓
            3 Research Log
            every variant you run
```

## The four folders you fill
They grow every week.

- **`1 Ideas/`** : the [[Idea Journal]] (one entry per idea, the day you meet it) and `Reading Notes/` (one note per source you read).
- **`2 Strategies/`** : one sheet per strategy, in two passes: the hypothesis before any code, what you measured after. The folder is the status: `Testing/`, `Live/`, `Parked/`, `Killed/`. The register is [[_Index]].
- **`3 Research Log/`** : every variant you run, and how much it costs you in luck. [[Research Log]]
- **`4 Portfolio/`** : the book ([[Strategy Table]]), one entry checklist and one monitoring note per strategy.

## The toolbox
- **`Toolbox/`** : what you open while you work. [[My Research Routine]] (the method as a weekly schedule) · [[Strategy Families]] · [[Instrument Traps]] · [[Reading Sources]] · [[Data Sources]] · [[Reading the Diagnostic]] · [[Deflated Sharpe]] · [[Glossary]]
- **`Templates/`** : a new reading note, strategy sheet, checklist or monitoring note. Create an empty note, then Cmd+P → *Templates: Insert template*.

## Where to start
1. Read [[My Research Routine]] once.
2. Open [[FOMC Drift]] and [[Crypto XS Momentum]]: two strategies filled end to end, one killed, one alive. That is the level of detail expected.
3. Open [[TSMOM 12-Month Trend]]: Pass 1 is written, Pass 2 is yours.
4. Pick an open entry in the [[Idea Journal]], or add your own, and fill Pass 1 of a new sheet before any code.

## The rule behind everything
Write what you expect before you look at the result. Every note in this vault exists to make that possible.
