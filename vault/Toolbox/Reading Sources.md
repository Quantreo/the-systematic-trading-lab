## Where to search
- **SSRN** : https://www.ssrn.com : search by family name ("time series momentum", "carry", "short-term reversal"), sort by downloads.
- **Quantpedia** : https://quantpedia.com/screener : a catalogue of strategies from papers, each with the family, the assets and the paper link. The free screener is enough to find ideas.
- **AQR Insights** : https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research : factor research written for practitioners.
- **Man Institute** : https://www.man.com/insights : CTA and multi-strategy views, strong on trend and regimes.
- **Alpha Architect** : https://alphaarchitect.com/blog : readable digests of academic papers.
- **Quantocracy** : https://quantocracy.com : daily aggregator of quant blogs.
- **Robot Wealth**, **Quantitativo**, **Rob Carver's blog** (https://qoppac.blogspot.com) : practitioners who show their work.

## Books
- Robert Carver, *Systematic Trading* (2015) : the framework used in this course (forecasts, FDM, vol targeting, buffering).
- Robert Carver, *Advanced Futures Trading Strategies* (2023) : 30 strategies, each with the mechanism and the numbers.
- Antti Ilmanen, *Expected Returns* (2011) : the encyclopedia of where returns come from.
- Andreas Clenow, *Following the Trend* (2013) : trend on futures, honest about drawdowns.
- Gary Antonacci, *Dual Momentum Investing* (2014) : simple, well argued.

## Fund letters (what actually worked, each year)
- Man AHL, Winton, Transtrend, Dunn Capital, Aspect Capital : annual and quarterly letters, usually public on their sites.
- Verdad Research, Newfound Research : short, quantitative, free.

## How to read
Four questions, always the same, in the [[Reading Note Template]]: what mechanism, on what data and period, what may have changed since, how would I test it with my data.
